Weekly Price Trends: July 2026 Market Analysis

Published: July 31, 2026 | By PricePoa Research Team

Key Findings

  • Maize flour (unga) prices decreased by 8% across all major chains due to improved harvest yields
  • Cooking oil showed volatility, with prices ranging from KES 245-280 for 2L containers
  • Sugar prices remained relatively stable, averaging KES 125-135 per kg
  • Milk prices increased slightly (3-5%) due to transportation cost adjustments
  • Best overall value: Carrefour for dry goods, Naivas for fresh produce

Shopping Recommendations

Where to Shop for Best Value

Based on our analysis of 10,000+ price points collected throughout July 2026:

  • For dry goods (flour, sugar, rice): Carrefour consistently offered the best prices, averaging 5-7% lower than competitors
  • For fresh produce: Naivas had superior quality and pricing for vegetables and fruits
  • For household essentials: Quickmart provided competitive pricing on cleaning supplies and toiletries
  • For specialty items: Chandarana excelled in organic and international food selections
  • For emergency/top-up shopping: Local dukas were convenient for small quantities, despite slightly higher per-unit prices

Looking Ahead: August 2026

Based on current market indicators and historical trends, we anticipate:

  • Continued stability in grain prices as harvest season progresses
  • Potential increases in vegetable prices due to seasonal transitions
  • Stable to slightly decreasing prices for cooking oil as global markets adjust
  • Increased promotional activity as stores prepare for back-to-school season

Pro Tip: Consider stocking up on non-perishables when you see favorable prices, and use PricePoa regularly to catch flash sales and promotional pricing.
