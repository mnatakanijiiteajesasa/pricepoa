Privacy Policy

How we protect your information

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Introduction

PricePoa ("we", "our", or "us") operates the PricePoa website and WhatsApp-based price comparison service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

By accessing or using our service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. Please read this policy carefully.

Information We Collect

PricePoa is designed to minimize data collection. We collect very limited information:

  • Messages sent via WhatsApp: Only the text content of your price comparison requests (e.g., "unga 2kg, sukari 1kg")
  • Usage Data: Anonymous, aggregated data about service usage (number of queries, popular items, average savings)
  • Technical Information: Standard web server logs for the website (IP addresses, browser type, referral pages)

What we DO NOT collect:

  • Your phone number or WhatsApp identifier
  • Personal identifiers (name, address, email)
  • Location data beyond general area for store matching
  • Shopping lists or purchase history
  • Payment information or financial data

How We Use Your Information

The limited information we collect is used solely to:

  • Process your price comparison requests in real-time
  • Provide accurate, up-to-date price information from partner stores
  • Improve our service through aggregated, anonymized analytics
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our platform
  • Communicate service updates (only if you opt-in through official channels)

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website or providing our service, provided those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

We may share aggregated, anonymized data with:

  • Partner stores for market analysis purposes (no personal identifiers)
  • Research institutions studying consumer pricing behavior (strictly anonymized)
  • Regulatory authorities when required by law

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Leverage WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption for all message transmissions
  • Implement strict access controls for any systems handling data
  • Regularly update and patch our security systems
  • Conduct periodic security assessments
  • Train our team on data protection best practices

However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to access, update, or delete information we have about you
  • The right to object to or restrict certain uses of your information
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to withdraw consent (where applicable)

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate actions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
