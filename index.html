How PricePoa Works

PricePoa operates entirely within WhatsApp's secure ecosystem. When you send a message:

  • Your message stays within WhatsApp's end-to-end encrypted channel
  • PricePoa processes your request in real-time without storing personal data
  • We check current prices from partner stores using anonymized, aggregated queries
  • Results are sent back to you through the same secure WhatsApp connection
  • No phone numbers, shopping lists, or personal identifiers are retained

Data Protection Commitment

We do not:

  • Store your phone number or WhatsApp ID
  • Keep records of your shopping lists or queries
  • Share data with third parties for marketing
  • Use tracking cookies or analytics that identify individuals

We do:

  • Process queries in real-time with instant results
  • Use aggregated, anonymized data for service improvement
  • Comply with WhatsApp's Business Policies and GDPR principles
  • Provide transparent service with clear opt-out options
Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Find quick answers to common questions about PricePoa.

Is PricePoa really free to use?

+

Yes! PricePoa is completely free to use. There are no hidden fees, subscriptions, or charges of any kind. We earn revenue through anonymized, aggregated data insights that help stores optimize their pricing - never from charging users.

How often are prices updated?

+

Prices are updated multiple times daily to ensure accuracy. We check prices at partner stores frequently throughout the day, with special attention to peak shopping hours. While we strive for real-time accuracy, we always recommend verifying prices at the store as a final check.

What areas does PricePoa cover?

+

Currently, PricePoa focuses on Nairobi and surrounding regions, with particular strength in Kasarani, Ruiru, Thika, and along the Mombasa Road corridor. We're actively expanding to other major Kenyan towns including Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, and Eldoret. Local dukas (small shops) are included based on your general location area.

Do I need to create an account?

+

No account needed! PricePoa works directly through your existing WhatsApp. Simply message our number and start comparing prices immediately - no registration, no login, no personal information required.

Is my data safe and private?

+

Absolutely. PricePoa leverages WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption, meaning your messages are secure from interception. We do not store your phone number, shopping lists, or personal data. All processing happens in real-time, and we follow strict data minimization principles - only using what's necessary to provide the service.
